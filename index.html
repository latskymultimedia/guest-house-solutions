What It’s About

Replace bulky, outdated physical folders and scattered WhatsApp messages with a lightning-fast, web-based guest portal that works directly on your guests' mobile phones without requiring a complicated app store download. It acts as a digital concierge right in the palm of their hand.

What’s Included

• Instant Arrival Access: One-tap digital check-in log and gate-opening integration.
• Seamless Amenities & Info: Instant access to Wi-Fi passwords, house manuals, rules, emergency numbers, and power/load-shedding status.
• Interactive Food & Bar Ordering: Complete digital menus (pizzas, drinks, mains) with live quantity counters, automated price totals, and direct ordering via WhatsApp.

• Professional Edge: Elevates a standard West Coast guest house experience to a high-end boutique hotel standard.

Pricing:

• Monthly Hosting & Maintenance Retainer: R500 – R1,000 / month
